How to read this
Fresh
Timed by our own harness in a controlled run: the same arguments every time, and in turn with a fixed reference commit in the same CI job (5 rounds each, the median kept). Every merge to main that touches the simulation gets one, and the backfill gave one to each merge since 14 October. Fresh points can be compared with each other.
Harvested
Numbers read back out of old CI logs: whatever arguments someone used that day, mostly on PR branches, with no shared reference. Drawn hollow, and hidden unless asked for: they are not comparable with the lines.
Reference
A fixed commit timed beside every measurement, so a slower or faster CI machine shows in both and the ratio between them stays comparable: eee3e81 for the GDScript era, the bridge for the cargo era.
Bridge (8ca4227)
The last commit with both the old GDScript benchmark tools and the Rust (cargo) binaries. Before it, a tick could only be timed in GDScript; after it, only in Rust. Timing the bridge with both states how the two scales relate, which is why the lines break there instead of joining.
The battle measured
tick_phases (both eras): 4,096 a side in 4 lanes of 1,024, the daily bench's size, timed over 3 fight ticks after 10 to settle. formation_bench: one clash of 1,024 a side, its mid-fight average. The 25k set has its own chart, below.
× the reference
A record's value over the reference's whole tick, timed in the same job. CI's runners differ (the bridge took 64 ms on one CPU and 99 ms on another), and the ratio mostly cancels that out. A record with no reference (the oldest) is left out in this unit; "ms" shows it. For a gap of a few percent, keep to one CPU, or confirm it with a same-runner bench.
CPUs
The runner's CPU, as each record names it ("unknown" without one). Untick one to take its records out of every chart and the compare; the line joins the rest.
Gap
A phase the harness didn't measure, or that took under its printed resolution (0.005 ms). Shown as a gap, never as 0; a line breaks where a commit has no number.

24,576 (49,152 units), ms (log scale): the 25k set

Sizes on this page are a side, mirrored: 4,096 is 4,096 against 4,096. Each merge that touched the simulation times two shapes at 24,576: 24 lanes of 1,024, and one squad. The line is each tick's p95 over the battle, the thin band above it the p99. Dotted: estimate: b·x + c·x² from the grid (Decision 165), fitted to that commit's smaller sizes (1,024 to 12,288); a ring marks a merge whose estimate misses the measured mean by more than 10%. The goal lines are Decision 159's, in ms; "× the bridge" shows each point over the bridge timed beside it in the same job, and hides them. A point's shape is its runner's CPU, as in the CPU toggles above. Hover a point for its CPU, the miss and the quadratic share c·x²/t; click it for that commit's fitted curve over size. "Show harvested points" adds the 25k runs read from old logs, hollow and never fitted.

A tick replayed: the repeats beside the whole tick, in % of tick

Spec 47: each merge's inspection ticks, replayed live many times (bench-inspect), as orange dots, beside the same phase's lap from the whole tick (the 25k set's record of the same commit and battle, its settled series' mean) as the blue line. The gap shows how far to trust the fast number. The repeats guide; the whole tick judges (Q47). ⚠ marks a phase whose cost builds up across ticks, which reads smaller repeated than in the whole tick.
