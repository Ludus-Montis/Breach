How to read this
Fresh
Timed by our own harness in a controlled run: the same arguments every time, and in turn with a fixed reference commit in the same CI job (5 rounds each, the median kept). Every merge to main that touches the simulation gets one, and the backfill gave one to each merge since 14 October. Fresh points can be compared with each other.
Harvested
Numbers read back out of old CI logs: whatever arguments someone used that day, mostly on PR branches, with no shared reference. Drawn hollow, and hidden unless asked for: they are not comparable with the lines.
Reference
A fixed commit timed beside every measurement, so a slower or faster CI machine shows in both and the ratio between them stays comparable: eee3e81 for the GDScript era, the bridge for the cargo era.
Bridge (8ca4227)
The last commit with both the old GDScript benchmark tools and the Rust (cargo) binaries. Before it, a tick could only be timed in GDScript; after it, only in Rust. Timing the bridge with both states how the two scales relate, which is why the lines break there instead of joining.
The battle measured
tick_phases (both eras): 4,096 a side in 4 lanes of 1,024, the daily bench's size, timed over 3 fight ticks after 10 to settle. formation_bench: one clash of 1,024 a side, its mid-fight average. The 25k target isn't recorded.
Gap
A phase the harness didn't measure, or that took under its printed resolution (0.005 ms). Shown as a gap, never as 0; a line breaks where a commit has no number.
